# LifeGuard AI 🚨⚕️

LifeGuard AI is an advanced, fully-integrated personal health unified platform. It serves as a continuous companion for Physical Health, Mental Well-being, and Emergency Response. Designed with a stunning, premium glassmorphism UI, LifeGuard AI seamlessly adapts to your real-time biometric data to offer actionable insights and reliable safety nets.

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Dynamic Health Dashboard
*   **Body Battery & Energy Monitor**: Calculates your theoretical energy capacity based on your recent sleep, stress, and activity levels.
*   **Real-time Vitals Logging**: Quickly input your heart rate, steps, hydration, and sleep to instantly update your dashboard graphics.
*   **AI Engine Insights**: Provides contextual warnings and suggestions based on your biometric fluctuations.

### 2. Emergency SOS Hub
*   **Global SOS Trigger**: One-tap emergency alert that triggers loud audio sirens, intense device vibrations (SOS morse code), and flashing screen elements.
*   **Instant Communication**: Automatically forwards your live GPS location to your "Safe Circle" contacts via **Twilio SMS**, **Automated Voice Calls**, and **Email**.
*   **Live Hospital Tracking**: Uses the Mapbox API to map your real-time coordinates and highlight the nearest medical facilities.

### 3. Mental Health Module
*   **AI Companion Chatbot**: A floating helper trained to provide empathetic responses, track emotional weight, and offer suicide-risk interventions.
*   **Clinical Assessments**: Interactive psychological questionnaires designed to gauge anxiety and depression levels.
*   **Relax & Reset**: Built-in interactive grounding exercises including "Bubble Pop", "Focus Tap", and guided "Breathing Circles" for immediate stress relief.

### 4. Physical Health Management
*   **Organ System Health Check**: An interactive diagnostic UI targeting specific core organs.
*   **Medication Reminders**: Specialized trackers to ensure consistency in medical regimens.
*   **Disease & Symptom Analyzer**: Detailed tracking of recurring symptoms for predictive analysis.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
*   **Frontend**: React.js (Vite)
*   **Styling**: Tailwind CSS & Framer Motion (for dynamic transitions and glass UI components)
*   **Iconography**: Lucide React
*   **Backend**: Node.js & Express
*   **Integrations**: 
    *   **OpenAI API** / **Groq** (AI processing)
    *   **Twilio** (SMS & Voice call automation)
    *   **Nodemailer** (Email dispatches)
    *   **Geolocation API** & **Mapbox** (Tracking)
